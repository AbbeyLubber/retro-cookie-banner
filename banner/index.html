Retro Cookie Banner

A free, open-source cookie consent banner in a single HTML file. No framework, no build step, no external requests: copy the file into any website and it shows a GDPR-style cookie popup with Accept All, Deny All and per-category toggles, then remembers the choice in localStorage. Source and documentation: github.com/AbbeyLubber/retro-cookie-banner.

This page is the live demo. English is the default language and is shown for any browser locale that is not listed below. Change your browser language and reload, or click one of these: ?lang=en-GB (English, default), ?lang=zh-HK (香港地區語言), ?lang=ru-RU (Русский язык), ?lang=uk-UA (Українська мова), ?lang=zh-SG (新加坡地区语言), ?lang=fi-FI (Suomen kieli), ?lang=de-DE (Deutsche Sprache), ?lang=be-BY (Беларуская мова), ?lang=et-EE (Eesti keel).

Changed your mind? reopens the dialog with the saved choice. That is RetroCookie.open(), the same call a "Cookie settings" link in your footer would make.
